In 2013,
[a fateful chain of posts](https://dispatchrabbi.tumblr.com/post/52352879845/woven-from-sapphire-fiber-robotamputee) was
made on Tumblr. Cousins had been connecting there virtually for some time, but it was time to plan the first in person
meetup. Originally pitched as an informal gathering at a pre-existing con, a group of Young Wizards fans quickly latched
onto the idea of having our own convention and started planning. Funded by an Indiegogo drive (that got almost twice as
much as we asked for), CrossingsCon came into being three years later in 2016 with our first convention in Newark, NJ,
USA. You can read more about that first con, and all our other past conventions,
[here]({% link _static/about/previous-cons.md %}).
