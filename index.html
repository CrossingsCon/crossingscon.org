Dai stihó, cousin!

CrossingsCon is where nerds from all universes gather to share experiences, meet friends both old and new, and stoke their passion for many fandoms. Enjoy panel discussions and interactive events led by Guests of Honor and other attendees, snag some fun merch, get a book signed by your favorite author, or maybe just hang out with them in the hallway! Originally inspired by Young Wizards, we invite you to join us at the Crossings, a gateway to any world.

Our most recent convention was in August of 2025. We are currently in between con cycles, but check back later and follow us on social media for more updates!
